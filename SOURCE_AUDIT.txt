CROSS-FILE CORRECTIONS FOR NUMERICAL / MEMORY MATERIAL
A. Synthetic addresses (0x1000, 0x500000, 0x800000) are teaching
   labels, NOT dumps from observed hardware or actual process mappings.
B. 64,000,000 bytes is decimal 64 MB, distinct from 64 MiB.
C. An ITCH A message is 36 bytes; the 32-byte synthetic in-memory
   order-record example is independent of ITCH wire layout.
D. 64-byte cache-line alignment does not by itself prove no sharing:
   inspect member offsets, adjacent objects and platform's destructive-
   interference granularity.
E. A 3 GHz assumed clock permits converting ideal cycles to ideal
   nanoseconds; it does not establish 14-cycle total handling, 1-cycle
   hash lookup, or universal 2-us mutex/allocations.
F. A page-table walk need not incur a DRAM access at every level.
G. mmap does not assign a user-specified PHYSICAL address. MAP_HUGETLB
   may fail or round allocation units. MAP_FIXED can replace mappings.
H. Cast-to-struct wire parsing must respect object lifetime, bounds,
   alignment, aliasing and byte order; zero copy is not a correctness proof.
I. A mutex lock does not always enter the kernel. std::map::find does
   not always miss the cache. Unmeasured stall constants are hypothetical.
J. std::pmr::monotonic_buffer_resource can request upstream memory.
   Preallocating an Order array does not guarantee global zero malloc.
K. std::memory_order_seq_cst defines ordering; it is not a promise to
   flush every CPU pipeline. [[likely]]/[[unlikely]] are C++20 attributes.
L. GCC __builtin_ctzll(0) has an undefined result; a mask scan needs
   separate handling of the zero mask.
M. isolcpus or pthread affinity alone do not prevent all interrupt,
   scheduling, SMT and OS interference.
N. Python/OS/C++ labs at universities are not proven identical to any
   HFT firm's private take-home without an employer primary document.
O. The NorthwestERN CS343 lab actually uses two producers/four consumers
   in an example, a 16-element ring and spinlock/mutex/semaphore tasks.
   Its 2025 PDF does not require implementing MCS, ticket, or lock-free
   MPMC algorithms. Signal-like interrupts are a separate lab condition.
